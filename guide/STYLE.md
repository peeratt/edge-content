# Writing guide

## Who we write for
- Owners and workshop managers of cabinet-making and joinery shops in Australia and New Zealand, typically 2–30 staff.
- They're practical, busy and sceptical of marketing. They read on their phone during a break.
- They're NOT homeowners. Skip kitchen design trends and DIY.

## The product (mention it naturally, never as a hard sell)
Edge Management App (edgemanagementapp.com) helps cabinet shops:
- measure what's left on edge tape rolls
- track edge tape inventory and reorder before running out
- print labels (Brother QL/TD printers) and scan rolls with QR codes

Link to the signup or pricing page once, near the end. Don't quote prices in posts, because they change.

## Voice
- Australian English (colour, organise, metre, tonne). Use AUD.
- Write like an experienced industry rep talking to a shop owner over the bench: plain, direct, a bit of dry humour.
- Use shop-floor language: edgebander, tape, offcuts, nesting, panel saw, CNC, job sheet.
- Use short paragraphs and no fluff. Don't start with "In today's fast-paced world".

## Honesty rules (these protect SEO and reputation)
- **Never invent statistics, studies, customer names or quotes.** For a number, either use one from `notes/` or frame it as a worked example ("say a shop runs 40 rolls…").
- **Stay vendor-neutral on machinery.** Don't praise or knock any brand of machine (Biesse, Homag, SCM, etc.). Talk about features and decisions, not brands.
- Don't give safety-critical instructions as if they were authoritative. Point readers to the manufacturer's manual and SafeWork.
- **Never use the owner's name** in posts, social captions or anything else that goes to the outside world. Write workshop notes in the business's voice ("we"). If a named reference seems needed, ask first in the pull request.

## Structure of every post (built for Google AND AI assistants)
1. **Title**: built around a question or problem the reader would actually search for, under 65 characters.
2. **Answer first**: the first 2–3 sentences answer the question directly. AI tools quote this part.
3. **Body**: 700–1,100 words with `##` headings phrased as questions where it fits. Include at least one practical list, table or worked example.
4. **From the workshop**: a short section using a note from `notes/` if there's one available.
5. **FAQ**: 3–4 short questions and answers, also included in the front matter.
6. **Close**: a single sentence linking to the app where it's genuinely relevant.

## Topic groups (rotate through them)
1. Edgebanding and materials
2. Running a lean workshop
3. Shop business (quoting, margins, staff, growth)
4. Machinery and software (general, vendor-neutral)

Aim for roughly half the posts to tie directly to edge tape, stock or labelling.
