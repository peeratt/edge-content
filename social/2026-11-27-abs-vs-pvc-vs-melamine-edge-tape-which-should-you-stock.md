# Social: ABS vs PVC vs melamine edge tape: which should you stock?

## Instagram
White 1 mm ABS. White 1 mm PVC. Can you tell them apart on the rack without a label?

Stocking both plastics doubles your roll count and doubles the chance of grabbing the wrong one. For most shops:
- ABS (or PVC, if you already run it well) for visible, high-wear edges
- Melamine for hidden edges and budget work
- Pick ONE main plastic, not two

Then label every roll with material, colour, thickness and what's left on it, so you stop reordering tape you already own.
