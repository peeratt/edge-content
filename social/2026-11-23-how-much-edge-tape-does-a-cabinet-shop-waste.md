# Social: How much edge tape does a cabinet shop really waste?

## Instagram
Your edgebander isn't the biggest tape waster in your shop.

The trim off each panel is small and predictable. The real waste is the roll you reorder because nobody could find the one already on the rack.

That's a triple loss: the duplicate tape, the extra delivery charge, and the time spent looking for it.

Fix it with three habits:
1. Label every roll
2. Record how much is left and where it lives
3. Check before you order

Full breakdown, including a worked example of machine trim vs duplicate orders, on the blog (link in bio).
