# Social: How to work out how much edge tape is left on a roll

## Instagram
"Yeah, there's heaps left on that one." Famous last words.

A roll that looks half full isn't half its length. The outer wraps hold far more tape than the ones near the core, so the eye guesses wrong every time.

Two ways to get a real number in under a minute:
1. Measure the outer and core diameter and run the formula
2. Weigh it and compare against a full roll

Then write the length on the label every time the roll comes off the edgebander.

Worked examples for both methods on the blog (link in bio).
