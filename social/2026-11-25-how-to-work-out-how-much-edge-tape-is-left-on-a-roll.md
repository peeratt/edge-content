## Instagram
"Yeah, there's heaps left on that one." Famous last words.

A roll that looks half full isn't half its length. The outer wraps hold far more tape than the ones near the core, so the eye guesses wrong every time.

All you need is a steel rule you already own:
1. Measure the outer diameter and the core
2. Run the simple formula
3. Get a real length in about a minute

No outlay, so you can start organising your tape today. Then write the length on the label every time the roll comes off the edgebander.
